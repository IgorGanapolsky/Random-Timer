# Gym Outreach Strategy — Broward County First

## Philosophy
Ask for feedback, not promotion. Coaches respond to respect, not pitches.

## Target: 10 Gyms in Broward County

### Tier 1 — Boxing / MMA (highest alignment)
1. Boxing gym where you train
2. Hard Knocks 365 (Coral Springs)
3. American Top Team (Coconut Creek)
4. Fight Sports (Fort Lauderdale)
5. Sanford MMA (Deerfield Beach)

### Tier 2 — BJJ
6. Gracie Barra (Coral Springs / Boca)
7. Atos Jiu-Jitsu (Fort Lauderdale)
8. Alliance BJJ (South Florida)

### Tier 3 — Tactical / LE
9. Local PD defensive tactics instructor (reach via LinkedIn)
10. Krav Maga / combatives school (Coral Springs / Boca)

## Outreach Message (DM or In-Person)

> Hey coach, I built a free reaction timer for fight drills. It fires at random intervals so trainees can't anticipate it — used for boxing flurries, BJJ scramble resets, and stress inoculation. If you want to try it in a class, I'd love your feedback. Here's the link: [App Store URL]

**Key**: "I'd love feedback" not "please promote." Big psychological difference.

## Drill Presets to Share

| Drill | Range | Alert Duration | Use Case |
|---|---|---|---|
| Boxing Flurry | 30s - 90s | 10s | Explosive combo + movement |
| BJJ Scramble | 20s - 2min | 15s | Intensity spike, reset position |
| Wall Work | 45s - 3min | 10s | Clinch pressure drill |
| Stress Conditioning | 1min - 5min | 30s | Extended unpredictable rounds |
| Dry Fire | 10s - 60s | 5s | Draw and present (safe training) |

## Review Collection (After 2 Weeks)

Target: 5-15 niche-quality reviews.

Message to users who tried it:
> "If the timer helped your training, a short App Store review really helps other fighters find it."

Do NOT scale paid ads before having 10+ quality reviews.

## Timeline

| Week | Action |
|---|---|
| 1 | DM 5 gyms. Record promo video at your gym. |
| 2 | Follow up. DM 5 more. Share video in fight groups. |
| 3 | Collect feedback. Ask for reviews. |
| 4 | Assess: which gyms adopted? What drill presets work? |
| 5+ | Scale what worked. Drop what didn't. |
