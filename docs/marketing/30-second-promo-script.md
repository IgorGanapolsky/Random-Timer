# 30-Second Promo Script

## Format
Vertical video (9:16). Shot at gym. No fancy editing — raw and real.

## Script

**[0-3s]** HOOK — Camera on you, mitts or heavy bag in frame.
> "Your timer is making you predictable."

**[3-8s]** PROBLEM — Quick cut, you glancing at a countdown timer.
> "Every round timer counts down the same way. You know exactly when it's coming. That's not how a real fight works."

**[8-15s]** SOLUTION — Show phone with app, tap Start.
> "Random Tactical Timer fires at a random moment in your range. You set 30 seconds to 5 minutes — and you have no idea when it hits."

**[15-22s]** DEMO — Shadowboxing or mitt work. Alarm goes off mid-combo. You react.
> "Now you're training reaction. Not anticipation."

**[22-27s]** PROOF — Quick montage: boxing drill, BJJ scramble reset, wall work.
> "Used by fighters, BJJ guys, law enforcement, and anyone who trains for the real thing."

**[27-30s]** CTA — App icon on screen.
> "Free on App Store and Google Play. Link in bio."

## Variations

### Gym Partner Version (15s)
> "My training partner doesn't know when this goes off. Neither do I. That's the point."
> [Show both reacting to alarm mid-drill]

### Self-Defense Dad Version (15s)
> "I'm 46. I train twice a week. I don't need a round timer — I need something that keeps me sharp when I'm not expecting it."
> [Show home garage gym setup with app]

### Instructor Version (15s)
> "I use this in my classes. Nobody knows when the next drill starts. Keeps everyone honest."
> [Show class reacting to alarm]
