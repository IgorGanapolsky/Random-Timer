Random Tactical Timer adds unpredictability to your routines. Set a time range, and the timer will go off at a random moment within that window. You never know exactly when it will ring — keeping you sharp and engaged.

PERFECT FOR:
- Workout intervals that keep your muscles guessing
- Games, drills, and team activities that need surprise timing
- Breaking predictable patterns in training or practice
- Meditation and mindfulness sessions with organic transitions
- Cooking timers when you want approximate reminders

KEY FEATURES:
- Custom time ranges — set your minimum and maximum duration
- Hidden mode — hides the countdown so you can't anticipate the alarm
- Loop mode — automatically restarts with a new random duration
- Lock screen countdown — see your timer right on the lock screen
- Sound and vibration alerts with adjustable volume
- Multiple alarm sounds (intense or gentle)
- Dark mode support
- Pause, resume, and reset controls
- Works in background with reliable foreground service
- Bluetooth and Android Auto alarm dismiss support

HOW IT WORKS:
1. Set your minimum and maximum time (e.g., 1-5 minutes)
2. Press Start — the app picks a random time in that range
3. Go about your activity — the timer runs in the background
4. When time's up, you get a full-screen alarm with sound and vibration

DESIGNED FOR ATHLETES, COACHES, AND ANYONE who needs unpredictable timing. Whether you're doing interval training, running reaction drills, or just want a timer that keeps you on your toes — Random Tactical Timer delivers.

No ads. No tracking. No subscriptions. Just a timer that works.
