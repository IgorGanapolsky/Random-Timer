Random Tactical Timer picks a random moment within your chosen time range to fire an alarm. Set a minimum and maximum duration, press start, and go about your activity. You never know exactly when it will go off — keeping you sharp, alert, and engaged.

Whether you need a random interval timer for HIIT training, a surprise countdown for party games like Musical Chairs or Hot Potato, or an unpredictable break timer for study sessions — this app delivers.

WHO IT'S FOR:
- Athletes & coaches doing interval training, Tabata, or reaction drills
- Gamers & party hosts running games that need surprise timing
- Students & professionals using randomized focus/break cycles
- Meditators wanting organic, non-predictable session transitions
- Anyone who needs a timer that doesn't let them anticipate the alarm

KEY FEATURES:
- Custom time ranges — set your minimum and maximum duration
- Hidden countdown — hides the timer so you can't anticipate the alarm
- Loop mode — automatically restarts with a new random duration each time
- Lock screen countdown — see your timer right on the lock screen
- Multiple alarm sounds — choose between intense and gentle alerts
- Adjustable volume and vibration controls
- Pause, resume, and reset at any time
- Runs reliably in the background with foreground service
- Works with Bluetooth headphones and Android Auto

HOW IT WORKS:
1. Set your minimum and maximum time (e.g., 30 seconds to 5 minutes)
2. Tap Start — the app picks a random duration in that range
3. Go about your activity — the timer counts down in the background
4. When time's up, a full-screen alarm fires with sound and vibration
5. Optionally loop to automatically start a new random interval

USE CASES:
- HIIT & Tabata workouts with unpredictable rest periods
- Reaction time training for martial arts, boxing, and sports
- Musical Chairs, Hot Potato, and other party games
- Pomodoro-style study sessions with randomized breaks
- Meditation with natural, non-rhythmic interval bells
- Cooking timers when approximate timing is fine
- Military and tactical training drills

DESIGNED FOR SIMPLICITY:
Dark interface. No clutter. No ads. No tracking. No subscriptions. Just set your range and go.
