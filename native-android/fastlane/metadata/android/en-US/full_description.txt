Random Tactical Timer fires an alarm at an unpredictable moment within your chosen time range. Set a minimum and maximum duration, press start, and train. You never know when it hits — keeping you sharp, reactive, and combat-ready.

Built for fighters, coaches, and tactical athletes who need to train reaction under real pressure — not predictable countdown patterns.

WHO IT'S FOR:
- Boxers, MMA fighters, and BJJ practitioners drilling reaction speed
- Self-defense practitioners training stress inoculation
- Coaches running unpredictable interval sessions
- CrossFit and HIIT athletes breaking predictable rest patterns
- Military and law enforcement doing tactical drill timing
- Anyone who needs a timer that eliminates anticipation

KEY FEATURES:
- Custom time ranges — set your minimum and maximum duration
- Hidden countdown — hides the timer so you can't anticipate the alarm
- Loop mode — automatically restarts with a new random duration each time
- Lock screen countdown — see your timer right on the lock screen
- Multiple alarm sounds — choose between intense and gentle alerts
- Adjustable volume and vibration controls
- Pause, resume, and reset at any time
- Runs reliably in the background with foreground service
- Works with Bluetooth headphones and Android Auto

HOW IT WORKS:
1. Set your minimum and maximum time (e.g., 30 seconds to 5 minutes)
2. Tap Start — the app picks a random duration in that range
3. Move, drill, spar, or work normally
4. React when the alarm fires with full-screen alert, sound, and vibration
5. Loop to automatically start a new random interval

USE CASES:
- Reaction drills for boxing, kickboxing, Muay Thai, and MMA
- BJJ position change timing and scramble training
- Stress inoculation for self-defense and combatives
- HIIT and conditioning with unpredictable intervals
- CrossFit EMOM and AMRAP with surprise transitions
- Military and tactical training scenarios
- Coach-led team practices with random whistle timing

DESIGNED FOR SIMPLICITY:
Dark interface. No clutter. No ads. No tracking. No subscriptions. Set your range. Train for chaos.
