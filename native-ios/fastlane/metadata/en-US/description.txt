Train reaction, not rhythm.

Random Tactical Timer fires a start cue at an unpredictable moment inside your chosen range, so you train response speed instead of anticipation.

Built for:
- Boxing, kickboxing, Muay Thai, MMA, and BJJ
- Bag work, pad rounds, sparring prep, and scramble drills
- HIIT and tactical conditioning sessions

Why athletes and coaches use it:
- Random cue between your minimum and maximum time
- Optional hidden countdown to reduce anticipation
- Loop mode for repeat rounds and interval blocks
- Loud alarm, vibration, and adjustable cue volume
- Quick controls: start, pause, resume, stop, and reset
- Live Activity support to keep active sessions visible

How it works:
1. Set your minimum and maximum time.
2. Start training normally.
3. React when the cue fires.
4. Repeat for consistent pressure training.

Fixed countdowns can create anticipation. Randomized cues help practice response under uncertainty.
