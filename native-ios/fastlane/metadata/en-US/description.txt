TRAIN REACTION, NOT RHYTHM.

Random Tactical Timer gives you a start cue at an unpredictable moment inside your chosen range. No fixed countdown. No easy pattern. Just real reaction training under pressure.

DESIGNED FOR:
- Boxing, kickboxing, Muay Thai, MMA, and BJJ drills
- Bag work, pad rounds, scramble transitions, and spar prep
- Tactical conditioning and stress-focused intervals
- HIIT sessions that need surprise start triggers

WHAT MAKES IT DIFFERENT:
- True random trigger between your min and max time
- Optional hidden countdown mode to prevent anticipation
- Loop mode for continuous multi-round training blocks
- Clear alarm + haptic vibration + adjustable volume
- Fast controls: start, stop, pause, resume, reset
- Live Activity support for active round visibility

USE IT IN 5 STEPS:
1. Set a range (example: 10s to 4m)
2. Press Start
3. Move and drill normally
4. React immediately when the cue hits
5. Repeat with loop mode for sustained stress rounds

If your current timer teaches anticipation, it is teaching the wrong habit. Train with uncertainty so your reaction speed holds up when timing is not predictable.
