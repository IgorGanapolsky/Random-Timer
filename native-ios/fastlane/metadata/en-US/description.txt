Stop training in predictable patterns.

Real fights aren't timed in neat 3-minute rounds. They explode without warning.

Random Tactical Timer creates unpredictable interval alerts so you can train reaction, awareness, and stress response under pressure. This is stress inoculation — the same method used by military, law enforcement, and combat athletes to build composure under fire.

Set your range. Press start. React when it hits. You never know when.

PERFECT FOR:
• Boxing reaction drills
• BJJ scramble rounds and pattern interrupts
• Wall work and clinch training
• Self-defense scenario drills
• Law enforcement defensive tactics
• Stress inoculation workouts
• HIIT and combat conditioning

WHY RANDOM TIMING?
Predictable timers create predictable brains. This app forces you to:
• React without anticipation
• Stay mentally present
• Adapt under uncertainty
• Build composure under stress

KEY FEATURES:
• Custom time ranges — set your minimum and maximum duration
• Hidden countdown — remove anticipation bias
• Loop mode — automatically restarts with a new random duration each time
• Live Activity — track state from the iPhone lock screen
• Multiple alarm sounds — choose between intense and gentle alerts
• Adjustable volume and vibration controls
• Pause, resume, and reset at any time
• Runs reliably in the background with push notifications

HOW IT WORKS:
1. Set your minimum and maximum time (e.g., 30 seconds to 5 minutes)
2. Tap Start — the app picks a random duration in that range
3. Go about your activity — the timer counts down in the background
4. When time's up, a full-screen alarm fires with sound and vibration
5. Optionally loop to automatically start a new random interval

Train for chaos. Not comfort.
