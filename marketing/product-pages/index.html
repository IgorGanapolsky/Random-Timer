FAQ for skeptical visitors

  • Is this just another interval timer? No. The app selects a random trigger time inside your chosen range, which removes the “I know it’s coming” problem.
  • Can I use it without paying? Yes. You can start free and preview the countdown cue plus a neutral voice sample before deciding whether Pro spoken countdown cues are worth it.
  • What should I do first? Start with one 5-minute session and aim to complete three drills in seven days. That is the habit threshold we care about.
  • Does it support mobile lock screens? Yes. It is designed to keep the timer alive while your phone is locked.
