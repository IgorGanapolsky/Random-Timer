# Release 1.3.44

## Summary
**1.3.44** restores the proven Play billing layout: monthly paywall SKUs query **`elite_tactical` + P1M**, not the legacy `elite_tactical_monthly` product id.

## Product
- **Android:** `playBillingProductId()` maps `elite_tactical_monthly` → `elite_tactical` for BillingClient queries and purchases.
- **Telemetry:** PostHog build `1780254806` still showed `billing_product_not_found` (response `-2`) for all SKUs when querying separate monthly product ids.

## Release operations
- Ship via internal-distribution → Play internal → `play-promote-to-production` (or `native-release` after firebase signoff).
