# Release 1.3.47

## Summary
Android billing catalog telemetry now reports `catalog_query_failed` with `probe_blocked_reason=network_error` when Play `queryProductDetails` exhausts retries with `NETWORK_ERROR`, instead of misleading `empty`. Caps `billing_product_query_retry` telemetry to three events per SKU per session.

## Product
- **Android:** Per-SKU query failure reasons flow into `resolveBillingProductCatalogStatus`; all-required-SKU network failures emit `catalog_query_failed`, not `empty`.
- **Android:** Partial catalog still reports `missing_required_products`; true empty catalog (no network failure) unchanged.
- **Telemetry:** `shouldEmitProductQueryRetryTelemetry` limits retry noise in PostHog.

## Release operations
- Requires active Play products: `elite_tactical` (annual + monthly base plans), `pro_base` (one-time).
- **Do not claim billing revenue or purchase success fixed** — verify `billing_product_catalog_status: ok` and paywall funnel on 1.3.47+ in PostHog after rollout.
