# Release 1.3.42

## Summary
**1.3.42** fixes Android monthly subscription purchases by billing `elite_tactical_monthly` where Play Console has the ACTIVE monthly base plan (corrects #1637 routing to `elite_tactical`, which only has annual ACTIVE).

## Product
- Monthly paywall queries and purchases use Play SKU `elite_tactical_monthly`.
- Play IAP verify accepts monthly on `elite_tactical_monthly` or `elite_tactical` P1M.
