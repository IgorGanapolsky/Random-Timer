# Release 1.3.41

## Summary
**1.3.41** fixes Android monthly subscription purchases by billing through `elite_tactical` P1M instead of the orphan Play product `elite_tactical_monthly`. Play IAP verify now fails releases when `elite_tactical` lacks active annual and monthly base plans.

## Product
- **Android paywall:** Monthly plan queries and purchases use `elite_tactical` + P1M offer (`ProManager.playBillingProductId`).
- **Release gate:** `play_verify_iap_products.py` blocks deploy when elite subscription base plans are not ACTIVE.

## Release operations
- Required Play subscriptions: `elite_tactical` with **annual** and **monthly** base plans ACTIVE; `pro_base` one-time ACTIVE.
- `elite_tactical_monthly` may remain in Console as legacy; app does not bill through it.
