# Release 1.3.45

## Summary
Restores Android Play Billing catalog queries for paywall purchases: monthly plan uses `elite_tactical` subscription P1M (per 1.3.41), correct SUBS vs INAPP types, retries, and PostHog billing diagnostics.

## Product
- **Android:** `playBillingProductId` maps `elite_tactical_monthly` → `elite_tactical`; `billingProductTypeForLogicalProductId` queries SUBS for elite/monthly and INAPP for `pro_base`.
- **Android:** Skips `queryProductDetails` when Play Store reports `PRODUCT_DETAILS` unsupported; tracks `billing_client_setup`, query retries, and `billing_response_label`.
- **Ops:** `marketing/data/posthog_observability.json` + `scripts/posthog_observability_bootstrap.py` for billing HogQL saved queries.

## Release operations
- Requires active Play products: `elite_tactical` (annual + monthly base plans), `pro_base` (one-time).
- After deploy, verify PostHog: `billing_product_catalog_status` → `ok`, `paywall_purchase_attempt` > 0 on new `$app_build`.
