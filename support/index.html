Frequently Asked Questions

How does the random timer work?

Set a minimum and maximum time range. The timer fires at a random moment within that window, forcing genuine reaction under stress instead of anticipating a countdown.

What is Pro mode?

Pro unlocks extended timer range (up to 60 minutes), AI voice callouts, loop mode with round limits, and the full sound library. It is available as an annual subscription.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On iOS: Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions > Random Tactical Timer > Cancel.
On Android: Play Store > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions > Random Tactical Timer > Cancel.

The timer stops when I switch apps

On Android, the timer runs as a foreground service and should continue in the background. On iOS, a notification will fire when the timer completes even if the app is backgrounded.
